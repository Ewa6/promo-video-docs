# Review and approve the storyboard

Claude shows you the storyboard after the interview and research. This review is the first of two approval points. Use it to agree on the words and the pictures before anyone records a real voice. Changes are cheap at this stage and cost nothing in ElevenLabs.

## What Claude shows you

For each cut, Claude gives you:

- **The script**: the exact text the voice will read. If you generate the voice yourself, Claude then gives you the final version to paste into ElevenLabs. Words in square brackets, such as `[Warm, assured]`, are directions for the voice. They aren't spoken and don't appear in captions.
- **The storyboard image**: one still frame for each line of the script, showing the picture when that line finishes. On Windows, you might get the frames as separate pictures. You can review them the same way.
- **The sources table**: where each line of the script comes from, such as your website or one of your answers.
- **A draft video**: a lower-quality version of the video. On a Mac, it has a robotic guide voice. The guide voice shows the timing, not how the final video will sound. On other systems, it's silent, and its timing is estimated.

## Review the storyboard

Check each part of the storyboard now, especially the wording.

1. Read the script aloud, or listen to the draft. Check that every claim is true and that the tone fits your brand.
2. Check the sources table. Every claim and number should come from a source you trust.
3. Look at the storyboard image. Check that each frame shows what its line talks about.
4. Watch the draft video. Check the order of the scenes and the pace.
5. Note anything you want changed. Be specific, for example, "Use 'make a board' instead of 'get started'," or "Show the print preview instead of the board editor."

> [!TIP]
> Changing the words after the voice is recorded means recording that cut again. Make wording changes now.

## Approve or ask for changes

When you finish your review, reply to Claude. Nothing moves on to the voiceover until you approve.

- To ask for changes, describe them in your reply. Claude updates the script and scenes and shows you a new storyboard.
- To approve, reply that you approve the storyboard. Claude then moves on to the voiceover.
